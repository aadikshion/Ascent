Methodology

Scores mirror the criteria Ascend publishes: market cap, unique holders, time-weighted volume and checks for concentrated or coordinated accumulation. Ascend's exact thresholds are internal and unpublished, so Ascent's targets are estimates and are fully configurable. This is a decision aid, not an official ruling.

Clustering uses on-chain transfers only: wallets receiving from the pool in the same block, and wallets funded by one common sender, are merged into one entity. Contract holders (pools, hooks, lockers) are excluded. Wallets holding under 0.001% of supply are ignored as dust.
